About Me

I am a Lecturer at the School of Computer and Electronic Information, Nanjing Normal University, Nanjing, China.

My research interests include evanescent-wave illumination microscopy and microsphere-assisted super-resolution imaging.

I received my B.S. degree from Henan Normal University in 2016, my M.S. degree from Nanjing Normal University in 2019, and my Ph.D. degree from Southeast University in 2023.
